An AR game for android devices utilizing Vuforia AR engine to bring story to life.

Video demonstration available on youtube at:

[![Watch the video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/jO0FEWp1H6E/hqdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/jO0FEWp1H6E)

## Controls 
### Keyboard on pc - 

V - attack

B - block

arrow keys - movement

### Touch controls on android - 
Right circle - joystick - movement

Left bottom circle - jump

Left upper circle - Attack

Left upper right circle - Block

## Devices
Works on pc & android devices. Requires camera which needs to be aimed at the AR Target.
